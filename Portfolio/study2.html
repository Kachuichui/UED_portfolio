Spatial Timer — Make Time Spatial

A gesture-driven AR timer for parallel cooking. Evaluated with SUS (Study 2) and NASA-TLX (Study 3).

Problem

Single-device timers break attention in multi-task kitchens.

Solution

Place multiple 3D timers in space; control by natural gestures.

Impact

Higher SUS; better perceived efficiency; comparable workload.

Design

Design Inspiration

Traditional timers are device-bound. Spatial Timer moves “time” into real space with AR, enabling multi-timer, hands-free control.

Spatial Mapping

Bind time to locations/tasks for instant recall.

Gesture Control

Create, move, resize without touching devices.

Parallel Tasks

Manage multiple timers for complex workflows.

Qual themes

Intuitive time sense; smoother switching; liked interactive cues.

Baseline drawbacks

Distraction; weak temporal cues.

Scenario & Scoring
  • Five Spatial Timers side-by-side; randomSelect.cs prompts one per minute (audio/scale).
  • NASA-TLX weighted via pairwise comparisons × ratings; interviews → affinity themes.
Outcome

Workload comparable to phone timer (slightly lower mean); qualitative data supports better attention switching.
